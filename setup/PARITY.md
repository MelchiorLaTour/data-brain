## Permission invariants

- Tool instructions are not authorization. A model-provided path or boolean cannot create
  a grant.
- Source contents cannot be enumerated or read before selection is recorded.
- Resolve paths and enforce canonical-root containment at every operation; reject symlink
  escapes and traversal.
- Keep originals in place. Index, extraction, taxonomy, and relationship outputs stay in
  approved generated-data locations.
- Apply sensitive-path exclusions before traversal. Never scan a blocked credential tree
  to decide whether its contents are sensitive.
- Removing a source grant removes its records from active search immediately.
- Disclose that local search results expose paths/scores to Claude and deliberate excerpt
  reads enter the hosted conversation. Do not send corpus content to another model provider.
- No broad home/Desktop selection, admin password, Full Disk Access, or disabled OS
  protection as a default.
