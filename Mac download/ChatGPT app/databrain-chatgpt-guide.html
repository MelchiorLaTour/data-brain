DataBrain · ChatGPT · Mac

What DataBrain will not read

  • Folders named Resources/Sensitive, .git, node_modules, .obsidian, .ssh, .gnupg, .aws, Python environment folders (names starting with .conda or .venv, and site-packages), the unzipped DataBrain installer folder, and any file that is not .md, .txt, .pdf, .docx, .doc, .pages or .rtf.
  • Names are checked first, then text is checked on your Mac. These checks run on your Mac.
  • Anything that looks private is held back until you say it is fine: taxes, bank, ID, medical, payslips, passwords, keys, contracts, in English and French, and any text with an IBAN, card number, social security number or private key.
  • Scanned images such as IMG_2231.jpg are not checked.

Do this:

  1. 1 →First, turn off Improve the model for everyone, before you download anything. Open ChatGPT → Settings… → Data controls, find Improve the model for everyone and switch it off. [CHECK]We recommend off: when it is on, your chats can be used to train OpenAI's models, and the passages DataBrain returns to answer you become part of your chat. DataBrain does not change this setting itself.
  2. 2 →Download the installer. On GitHub, open the data-brain page, then the Mac download folder, then ChatGPT app, and click the download link in the heading (Click here to download DataBrain-ChatGPT-Installer-0.2.1.zip). Or use this direct link: DataBrain-ChatGPT-Installer-0.2.1.zipDo not click the file name in the list below the heading: that only opens a preview page, it does not download. The file lands in your Downloads folder. You need the ChatGPT app, with a Plus plan or higher, on a Mac with an Apple chip.
  3. 3 →Open Downloads (in the Finder, in the left list). Double-click DataBrain-ChatGPT-Installer-0.2.1, then double-click Install DataBrain for ChatGPT. [CHECK]If Downloads shows a .zip file and no folder, double-click the zip first. Your Mac then makes the folder.
  4. 4 →Your Mac blocks the file the first time, because it comes from the internet and is not signed with a paid Apple certificate. Click Done. Then open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll to the bottom, click Open Anyway next to Install DataBrain for ChatGPT.command, type your Mac password, and click Open. [CHECK]You do this once. It lets this one file run. It does not turn anything off for the rest of your Mac. The name of the button and the wording of the message can differ a little between macOS versions.
  5. 5 →A Terminal window opens and runs the installer. Wait until it says DataBrain for ChatGPT 0.2.1 is installed., then press Return to restart ChatGPT. [CHECK]It copies DataBrain into your Library folder and adds one entry for DataBrain to ChatGPT's settings file (~/.codex/config.toml), after saving a copy of that file. It also adds one small file, ~/.agents/skills/databrain-save-note/SKILL.md, that tells ChatGPT to save your notes through DataBrain. It never opens your documents. If you would rather restart yourself, press Command-Q in ChatGPT and open it again. If it says a different tool named databrain is already there, it stops without changing anything.
  6. 6 →Check that DataBrain loaded. In ChatGPT, open Settings → MCP servers: databrain should be listed and switched on. [CHECK]Close Settings completely and open a new chat. You may need to scroll the list to find it.
  7. 7 →In a new chat, send this message:
    DataBrain reads nothing yet. A box opens in the chat so you can choose the folders. [CHECK]
  8. 8 →In the box, leave Desktop, Documents and Downloads ticked. Nothing gets moved. To include the apps you write in, like Notes, tick Applications. Type one more line in Other folders for every other top-level folder you want searched (see footnote 1). Then click Accept. These are all the folders your DataBrain can read. Sensitive information is held back and left untouched during setup. [CHECK]Your click on Accept is your yes, for exactly the folders ticked. Decline or closing the box changes nothing, and you can send the message again. A folder you do not want? Untick it. Only folders that exist on your Mac are listed. For Other folders, write the full path, or start with ~/, for example ~/Documents/Notes. If ChatGPT says a folder does not exist, check the spelling of the name. Applications shows the apps you installed and the ones that come with your Mac, together. Password managers are never read. To search other folders later, ask ChatGPT to add folders to DataBrain and use the box the same way.
  9. 9 →ChatGPT may tell you that some files are private and cannot be accessed. It never says which. To see them, open Private files review.txt in your DataBrain folder (Finder → Go → Home), change keep: no to keep: yes on the line of any file that is fine to index, save the file, and tell ChatGPT: I reviewed my private files. If you are not sure, do nothing: every file stays private. When ChatGPT asks to use DataBrain, click Allow. [CHECK]If the box offers a choice like Always allow, it saves you from approving each step of setup. The choice is yours.
  10. 10 →When your Mac asks to let ChatGPT access your Desktop or Documents folder, or to control an app like Notes, click OK (or Allow). [CHECK]It can appear while ChatGPT sets up. Documents, Desktop, Downloads, iCloud Drive, external drives, and each app each ask once. An app like Notes may open for a moment the first time DataBrain reads it, and DataBrain closes it again if it opened it. If you click Don't Allow for an app, DataBrain tells you where to turn it on later: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Automation. The permission applies to ChatGPT as a whole, not only to DataBrain.

When ChatGPT says DataBrain is ready, open a new chat and ask about something that is in your files. [CHECK]

That's all.

You can delete the downloaded zip and its folder now. They are in your Downloads folder. To update DataBrain later, download the new installer and open it the same way: your brain is kept.

DataBrain lives in your home folder: Finder → Go → Home.

To remove DataBrain: double-click Uninstall DataBrain for ChatGPT, next to the installer. It removes the program, its entry in ChatGPT's settings file and the note-saving file, and nothing else. By hand instead: in the Finder, click Go → Go to Folder…, type ~/Library/Application Support/DataBrain, press Return, and drag the app folder to the Trash. Then open ~/.codex/config.toml in TextEdit and delete the five lines that start at [mcp_servers.databrain]. Your own files are never touched. DataBrain's folder stays on your Mac, in your home folder (Finder → Go → Home), named DataBrain, or DataBrain 2 and so on. It holds copies of the text DataBrain read, so drag it to the Trash if you want that gone.

Footnote 1: which folders to add

For people who know computers: adding a directory gives DataBrain read access to that folder and everything under it, including all subfolders. Add the top-level (parent) folders you want searched, such as the ones in your Finder Favorites list: Desktop, Documents, Downloads. You do not need to add their subfolders. Do not add your whole home folder: DataBrain refuses it.

Like you are five: a folder is a box. A small box holds a few papers. A big box holds lots of little boxes. If you pick a big box, DataBrain looks inside all the little boxes in it too, so you do not have to pick each little box. But a big box like Documents has everything in it, even papers you may not want. If you only want one little box, pick just that one.

Your files are safe

  • DataBrain only reads your files. It never changes, moves, or deletes them.
  • The search index stays on your Mac, in the DataBrain folder.
  • Your files stay on your Mac. DataBrain has no cloud server and stores nothing of yours. On ChatGPT, only the query and the snippets needed to answer it are sent to OpenAI's model.
  • The passages ChatGPT reads go into your ChatGPT chat like any message you type, and when you allow a Mac app permission, it applies to ChatGPT as a whole, not only to DataBrain.

If something goes wrong

  • ChatGPT can't read your files: open System Settings → Privacy & Security → Files & Folders, turn on ChatGPT [CHECK], then send Continue setting up my DataBrain
  • ChatGPT says a folder does not exist, or that macOS does not let DataBrain read it: it names the folder. Check the spelling of the name, or turn on ChatGPT in Files & Folders as above. This is not a repair problem.
  • ChatGPT says a DataBrain folder already exists: do what ChatGPT tells you.
  • Anything else: send Run DataBrain diagnostics and pass ChatGPT's reply to whoever sent you this guide.

If ChatGPT does not answer at all, or DataBrain is not listed, these steps need no help from ChatGPT:

  • Quit ChatGPT with Command-Q and open it again.
  • Turn the tool on: ChatGPT → Settings… → MCP servers, and make databrain switched on. [CHECK]
  • Let your Mac share the folders: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Files & Folders, turn on ChatGPT.
  • Run the installer again, the same way as steps 3 to 5. DataBrain finds the brain it already made and keeps your folders; it does not start over, and your files are still searchable.
